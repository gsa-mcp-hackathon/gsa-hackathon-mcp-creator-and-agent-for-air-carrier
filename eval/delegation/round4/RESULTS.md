## How to read this

* **Not independent of the tools.** Genie Code wrote the generator's templates and drafted the 24 reviewed questions, and has seen the tools; the project owner reviewed and edited the 24. The generator removes hand-picking of subjects (a fixed seed drew them from the source tables), not authorship. A set written by someone who has not used the tools would be a stronger test.
* **Not comparable point-for-point with rounds 2 and 3** (43.1% and 69.4% with tools). Round 4 has different questions, a different mix (more single-figure lookups in the generated set) and a different grader (v2 parses numbers, units, dates and refusals; rounds 1-3 used regexes).
* **What changed between seal and run:** only the runner (checkpointing and retries, after the first attempt outlasted the command time limit and saved nothing). No tool, server or grader code changed. One unsaved timing probe ran the 69 questions once on GPT-5 mini before the official run; nothing was changed after it. See [SEAL.md](SEAL.md).
* **The no-tools baseline** is the same model with the same prompt and no tools. 8 of its 11 passes are premise traps or non-existent modifications, where saying a thing cannot be found is the right answer.
