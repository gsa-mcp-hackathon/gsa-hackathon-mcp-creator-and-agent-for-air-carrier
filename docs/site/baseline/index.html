Cost · Estimate history and caps

Cost growth, and what it actually is

The estimate moved from $9,192.0M to $13,409.970M across 12 published estimates, about +45.9%. A product that draws one line labelled "cost growth" will be wrong about four fifths of it.

The three ceilings in circulation, and the step-by-step bridge from the statutory cap to the budget submission, are on Position. This page is how the estimate got there over time.

Five distinct mechanisms, and only one is cost growth

Estimate revision with no scope change
The ship did not change; the estimate did
Statute moved and the estimate followed it
GAO: the cost cap informed the program cost estimate rather than the reverse
Genuine contract overrun
The only one of the five that is cost growth in the ordinary sense
Scope transferred into the baseline
The ship got more expensive; the program did not
Reclassification inside the program
GAO FY2026-27: not new program costs
A sixth mechanism is visible in the same exhibit family and matters for other hulls: "Less AP Transfer to NSBDF" lines show advance procurement moving out of SCN into the National Sea-Based Deterrence Fund. That is a cost leaving the appropriation without leaving the program.
