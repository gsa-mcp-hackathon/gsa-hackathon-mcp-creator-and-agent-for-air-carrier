Coverage map

Which layers are present, at what fidelity, and for the absent ones, what specific access would change that. This is the artifact that does the work in the room: it converts a statement of what is missing into a named ask.

The six layers

L2 is the hub, the only layer where every dollar has a posting. L1, L3 and L4 each tie to it on a different cut. L0 ties to L1 and L4 and never directly to L2. L5 sits beneath L2.

L0 · Reported

rich The Department to Congress, plus oversight bodies · Justification and accountability

Present

Parts A to E and K.2. SARs, MSARs, J-Books, CRS, GAO, DODIG

What would change it

none needed

L1 · Budget

partial The comptroller chain · Plan, request, appropriate, apportion, allot, obligate, expend

Present

P-5c categories, P-40 funding streams by year, the appropriation map from the contract's own account file

What would change it

Advana budget execution for the specific appropriation lines (Gate D)

L2 · Ledger

absent The accounting systems and DFAS · Record transactions under the USSGL

Present

nothing

What would change it

NAVSEA 01 or PEO Carriers. The long pole

L3 · Asset

standards only Accounting plus property accountability · Accumulate CIP, capitalize at placement in service, depreciate

Present

Part I. The standards stack and the three structural divergences. No figures

What would change it

Navy Office of Financial Operations

L4 · Program

partial PEO Carriers, PMS 379, the shipbuilder · Manage cost and schedule against a baseline

Present

MSAR EVM for CVN-80 at 56.43% complete, cost variance -$1,745.6M. Sister-hull only

What would change it

CADE / DCARC. Compliance rated Red

L5 · Evidence

partial Feeder systems · The underlying documents

Present

1,875 prime transactions and 2,990 sub-awards, held and parsed

What would change it

PIEE, EDA, MOCAS for the rest

The asks, in order of how cheap they are

  1. Two questions closable with one email each. Was any budget submitting office carrying CVN-79 funds ever on SABRS (G2, NAVSEA 01 or DFAS). Were GAO-17-575's per-hull cost reporting recommendations implemented (G4, NAVSEA 05C).
  2. Run the GFP Position Report for CAGE 43689 scoped to both contracts (G16). Existing report, defined key, mandated cadence, no new access path. Best ratio in the register.
  3. Confirm Advana access covers the specific appropriation lines funding this hull.
  4. Approve the egress allowlist for the named .gov event sources.
  5. One introduction, to NAVSEA 01 Comptroller and to PEO Carriers or PMS 379. This is the real ask. Access is the binding constraint and an introduction is the only instrument that moves it.
  6. Endorsement to publish the gap register department-wide, which is what opens the community of practice.
