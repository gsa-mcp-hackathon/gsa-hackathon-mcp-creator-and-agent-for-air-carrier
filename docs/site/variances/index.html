Variance register

Where public sources disagree. Each is a real finding about the reporting rather than a data error to clean. Conflicting values are retained with provenance and never resolved by selection: if the product is the variance, two sources disagreeing about the same cell is the raw material rather than a defect.

E-1 live

Three different cost caps in circulation

Statutory cap $11,398.0M, SECNAV Cost Limitation Baseline $12,936M, budget submission $13,409.970M. All three are current and all three are correct for their own purpose. Any product showing one number as the cost cap will be wrong. The baseline itself is under review: see E-17.

E-2 resolved

The $11.341B figure

A sourced FY2019 budget estimate in then-year dollars, not a total and not a cap. Usable as a dated estimate, never as the cost of the ship.

E-3 resolved

Obligation Work Limiting Date

Resolved in favour of OPNAVINST 4700.8M. CFO Jul 2027 plus eleven months gives Jun 2028. The NAVSEA desk reference reading of thirteen months after delivery would give Apr 2028.

E-5 live

Attributed delay cause shifts by year and by authority

The budget footnote says AAG certification and AWE. DOT&E adds supply chain. GAO adds material availability and workforce. HII adds untimely availability of Ford lessons. Same slip, four causes. That is itself a finding.

E-7 live

Is the FY2026-FY2027 unit cost increase new cost

GAO: unit costs up 3%, about $480M, roughly half from delivery schedule changes, but the Navy is using funds already budgeted for post-delivery, so these are not new program costs. The same dollars are a cost increase in one view and a reclassification in another.

The dagger inference

Part K.1 reasoned the dagger marked GFE-bearing categories. The exhibit defines it in place as indicating the presence of a P-8a. Plausible, convenient, consistent with everything else, and wrong. Kept because the shape recurs: a real structural pattern with an invented interpretation laid over it.

E-13 recorded error METHOD

Announced modifications summed as obligations

N00024-09-C-2116 was carried at roughly $1.08B, built by summing announced DoD contract modifications. The obligation record gives $4,519,300,323.83, an understatement by a factor of 4.18. Announcements have a dollar threshold, omit incremental funding (which carries $2.49B here) and cannot net deobligations (68 actions, -$66.8M). A press release is an announcement, never a record.

USAspending disagrees with itself

By exactly $5,880,250.00 on N00024-15-C-2114. Two of three products agree, so the transaction sum is canonical. Both are the same system on the same as-of date.

E-15 live KEY

The 2116 identifier collision

N00024-09-C-2116 is this hull's Construction Preparation. N00024-16-C-2116 is CVN-80 and CVN-81 DD&C, carrying a $14,917,738,145 modification. Same last four digits, seven years apart, different hulls. Any fuzzy or partial match merges three hulls undetectably.

E-16 resolved METHOD

FPDS-NG and USAspending differ by $583,963.00 on the construction preparation contract: an as-of difference

Resolved 2026-11-08. The contract is N00024-09-C-2116 (construction preparation), not the DD&C contract N00024-15-C-2114, whose transaction sum is $4,578,603,345.77. The FPDS-NG ATOM feed reported totalObligatedAmount $4,518,716,360.83 against USAspending's transaction sum of $4,519,300,323.83. The USAspending running total on 09-C-2116 through action date 2026-06-10 is exactly $4,518,716,360.83. The eight actions dated 2026-06-17 to 2026-07-20 (A00645 to AZ0122) sum to exactly $583,963.00. Cause: as-of date. totalObligatedAmount is cumulative per modification, and the entry read was the 10 June action, not the latest. Method rule: read totalObligatedAmount from the latest modification, and state the as-of date with every obligation figure. Caveat: the FPDS side is the figure as recorded on 2026-11-08 and was not re-pulled.

E-17 live KEY

A December 2025 Cost Limitation Baseline of $13,529M is printed but not yet confirmed

The FY2027 MSAR (CVN_78_MSAR_FY2027_PBv2.pdf, p.8) says that in December 2025 the Secretary of the Navy notified Congress he was increasing the CVN-79 Cost Limitation Baseline from $12.936B to $13.529B. The One Hub still carries $12,936M (August 2023) as the baseline in force, because the figure gate routed $13,529M to human review: it comes from an MSAR sentence, not the notification, with no adjustments or exclusions. Hull caution: $13.529B is also CVN 80's baseline since August 2023 (MSAR Dec 2023, p.7). If confirmed, the FY2027 budget total of $13,409.970M sits $119.03M under the baseline rather than $473.97M over it. Open with OUSD(C) FMO as the CAP-05 request; registered 2026-11-19 from evaluation finding F1.
