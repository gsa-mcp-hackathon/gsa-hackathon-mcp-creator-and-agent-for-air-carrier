The document families this product reads, what each uniquely carries, the limit on each, and the searchable corpus behind every citation. A source's limit is as important as its content: most of the recorded errors came from reading a document for something it does not contain.

Modernized Selected Acquisition Report

MSAR / SAR

Carries

contract numbers, CAGE codes, percent complete, EVM variances, funding by appropriation and line item, Cost Limitation Baseline history

Limit

Reports aggregate program cost for all ships in the class, not broken out by hull. Genuine CVN-79 detail at contract and subprogram level; no clean hull-allocated total.

Lexical retrieval over the indexed documents. Authoritative sources only by default: without that filter the analyst's own dossier outranks the documents it summarises on nearly every query, because a summary written in your vocabulary always out-matches its source. Citing the summary is how a lead silently becomes a figure.
