Which CVN-79 cost figure do I quote, and for what?

For senior leaders, the PEO, budget analysts Decision which figure goes in the brief, in what words, and which to leave out

Four figures are published for CVN-79 and none of them agree. Each is current, sourced and correct for its own purpose. Most of the disagreement is explained below; the part that is not is named, with what it would take to close it.

Selection: the three ceilings in variance E-1 and the FY2019 estimate in E-2. The December 2025 baseline in E-17 is held for review and is not one of the four. This is the app's reading of the register, not a figure cleared for use.

The bridge from the cap to the budget, step by step

Each increment, the estimate it lands on, who published it, and the mechanism the estimate history records for it. Only one mechanism is cost growth in the ordinary sense. The FY2019 estimate is not on this chain: A sourced FY2019 budget estimate in then-year dollars, not a total and not a cap. Usable as a dated estimate, never as the cost of the ship.

The chain closes exactly, from the statutory cap at $11,398.0M to the budget submission at $13,409.97M, $2,011.97M (17.7%) above the cap. 2 of its 4 increments are derived rather than read from a document ($238M, $260M), so the arithmetic is closed and the attribution of those two steps is not.

$213.97M of the overrun is contested (E-7). GAO: unit costs up 3%, about $480M, roughly half from delivery schedule changes, but the Navy is using funds already budgeted for post-delivery, so these are not new program costs. The same dollars are a cost increase in one view and a reclassification in another. The register does not reconcile GAO's figure with the budget increment.

The difference that cannot yet be explained

Is the $2,012M above the cap cost growth? Nobody can say yet. The cost cap statute excludes defined categories of cost, and which categories apply to this hull is open. Until it closes, the distance between the estimate and the cap is arithmetic, not a finding.

Who can close itNavy FMB or OGC

Why it mattersFY2016 NDAA cap exclusions. Governs the meaning of the E-1 three-ceiling spread the product intends to lead with.
And separately: $3,252M of the hull (24.2%) has no public contract record. The largest block is vendor level attribution of the propulsion equipment category, G22, for Naval Reactors, NAVSEA, Department of Energy to close (access required).
