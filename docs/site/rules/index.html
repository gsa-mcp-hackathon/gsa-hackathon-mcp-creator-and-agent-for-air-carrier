Rule codes

Every code used in this product, with the rule it stands for and where it is defined. Three families: the SI rulebook (task-level rules a specialist run cites), the rule register (exception classes that govern the work queue), and the gate routes (claim promotion decisions).

SI rulebook

The task-level rules every specialist run works under, shown exactly as the specialist receives them (app/api/si/tasks.py RULEBOOK). EC-01 and EC-02 are system rules: the gate and the canvas, not rules a specialist cites.

CodeDescription
OB-01 A contract's obligations are federal_action_obligation summed per action in exact decimal. total_dollars_obligated is a running total and is never summed.
OB-02 A contract total is stated with its PIID. N00024-09-C-2116 (construction preparation) and N00024-15-C-2114 (detail design and construction) are never labelled as each other (E-16).
OB-03 FPDS is the contract action record, not the accounting record. A reconciled FPDS total leaves the accounting-system total as a marked blank for the office that holds it.
TO-01 A File C period ties when one transaction window (three months to the period end, fiscal year to date, or the single month) matches its File C amount to the cent. Which window matched is reported, never assumed.
TO-02 A period that does not tie is a residual to explain, not an error to correct. The explanation names the account and the office that can confirm it.
GP-01 A gap is worked toward one collection request: the document, the system and the office the record names. A missing office is a marked blank.
BR-01 Only promoted claims (zone V) are stated as standing at the brief. Every other figure is a lead and says so.
BR-02 Every sentence in a brief draft cites a claim, variance or gap id. Nothing goes to the sponsor from here.
EC-01 Nothing is saved until a person applies it. system
EC-02 Every stated amount, count and date matches a packed record. system
EC-03 Where the record is silent, the draft keeps a marked blank and names who fills it.

Gate routes

The claim promotion gate (seed/gate.yaml), in evaluation order. A claim enters with a trust tag and a materiality flag, and the first route that matches decides: promote, hold, or send to a human. Copied into the app 2026-11-20T23:14:30+00:00.

R3 INTERPRETATION and below floor hold_candidate

R5 material and VERIFIED and unflagged promote_if_tied_out

Where each family lives. SI rulebook codes (OB, TO, GP, BR, EC) are read from app/api/si/tasks.py and enforced by the SI engine (app/api/si/sdk.py). Rule register codes (CC, EV, CAP, AV, CM) were seeded by deploy/seed_rules.py; later versions are inserted into the register, which is insert-only, and read here live. Gate routes are defined in seed/gate.yaml and applied by core/policy/gate.py. Nothing on this page is typed by hand.
