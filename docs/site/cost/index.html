Decomposition

The authoritative figure is $13,409.970M from the FY2027 submission. It decomposes to zero residual on two independent axes: by cost category and by funding stream. One axis closing is arithmetic. Two is evidence.

Two things here are counter-intuitive. EASR is budgeted under Ordnance, not Electronics: the P-40 systems list puts EMALS, EASR, AAG and MOVLAS under Ordnance and only SSDS under Electronics. Reading "Electronics 263.359" as the ship's electronics content understates it by a factor of five. And the dagger on three rows marks the presence of a P-8a exhibit, not government furnished content.

A quarter of the hull was bought before the ship was authorised. Advance procurement ran FY2007 to FY2012; the procurement year was FY2013 and carried 3.7%. Any timeline that starts at authorisation misses about $3.3 billion.

Attribution: what a named contract or vendor line accounts for

Attribution improved from 42.2% to 75.8% in one pass, entirely by replacing an announcement-derived figure with the obligation record. The residual is not a budget gap: the budget closes to zero twice over. It is the portion of the hull that no public contract-level record attributes to CVN-79.
