Industrial base

Who builds what

HII Newport News Shipbuilding is the hull builder and the systems integrator. It is not the manufacturer of most of the ship's major systems. That is why neither roll-up method is sufficient alone, and why cross-command government furnished equipment is the hardest attribution problem in this program.

Why the industrial map changes the cost architecture

The two HII contracts buy construction and integration, not content. Electronics, Propulsion Equipment and Ordnance are substantially government furnished, bought on other contracts by other commands.
Neither roll-up method is sufficient alone. A contract roll-up anchored on CAGE 43689 captures the hull and misses the systems. A category roll-up from the P-5 captures the systems but cannot see the contracts beneath them.
Cross-command GFE is where hull attribution breaks. An EMALS shipset bought by NAVAIR on an aviation contract and installed by HII on a NAVSEA ship under a NAVSEA contract has no single line of accounting that says CVN-79 from end to end. That, not the ledger seam, is the hardest attribution problem in this program.
