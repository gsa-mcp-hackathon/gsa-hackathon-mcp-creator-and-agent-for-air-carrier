Work queue

What needs a decision, what is blocked, and why

For the person who owns a record or holds a named role Decision apply, reject or send back each item, under your own identity

The queue answers one question for the person who owns a record: what must I decide, and is the evidence good enough to sign? Items land in one of three lanes by code, never by a model. Decide is work a named role can act on now. Unblock is what keeps rules from running, one row per root cause. System is test data, repeats and defects: real, but not a decision.
