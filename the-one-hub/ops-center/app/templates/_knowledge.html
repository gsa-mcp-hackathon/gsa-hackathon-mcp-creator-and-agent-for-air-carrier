Search it

Lexical retrieval over the indexed documents. Authoritative sources only by default: without that filter the analyst's own dossier outranks the documents it summarises on nearly every query, because a summary written in your vocabulary always out-matches its source. Citing the summary is how a lead silently becomes a figure.
