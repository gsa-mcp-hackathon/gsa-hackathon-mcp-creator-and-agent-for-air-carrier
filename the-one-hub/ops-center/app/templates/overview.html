The three sentences

One

The ship is ${{ "{:,.3f}".format(program.total_ship_estimate_usd_m) }}M and it decomposes exactly, two different ways. By category it is 63.1% basic construction, 15.2% propulsion equipment, 8.6% ordnance, 6.7% plans. By funding stream it is 24.8% advance procurement bought before the ship was authorised, 56.0% subsequent full funding, 15.6% cost to complete still running, and 3.7% in the procurement year itself.

Two

Two contracts with one company are {{ contracts.combined_share_of_hull }}% of the ship and {{ contracts.combined_share_of_shipbuilder_side }}% of everything the shipbuilder is paid out of. They are sequential rather than parallel and cross in FY2017. "Construction Preparation" built the ship: $2.49 billion of it is described as advance construction, not planning.

Three

The ship is ${{ "{:,.0f}".format(program.above_cap_usd_m) }}M above its statutory cost cap, {{ program.above_cap_pct }}%, and that overage is fully attributable to four dated movements, of which the $1.3 billion August 2021 contract overrun is the only one that is cost growth in the ordinary sense.
