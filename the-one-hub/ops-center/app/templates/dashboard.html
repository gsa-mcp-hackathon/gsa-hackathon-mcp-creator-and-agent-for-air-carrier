Position against every ceiling

Three ceilings are in circulation and all three are current. The bar is the current estimate, the vertical rule is the ceiling. A product that shows one of these as "the cost cap" is wrong for two of its three audiences.

The cap is not a budget line. The legislation excludes defined categories of cost, and which categories is still open (G12). Until that closes, the distance between the estimate and the cap cannot be read as cost growth.
