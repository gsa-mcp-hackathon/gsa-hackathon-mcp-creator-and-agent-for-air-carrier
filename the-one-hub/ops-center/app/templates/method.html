Method

How this is built, and why it can be challenged

The goal is not a single authoritative number. A single number tied to one declared source of truth is worth less than a defensible account of how the number is reported, how it is recorded, where those diverge, and why. The divergence with its cause attached is the product.

Reference: every rule code · the evidence coverage map · the stack and its evaluation

The design premise

The data is messy and the picture is never complete. That is the normal condition rather than an exception, and treating it otherwise is how this kind of effort fails. The architecture is an investigation apparatus before it is a reporting apparatus.

Completeness is never a gate

No component may require that a layer be present, that a system inventory be finished, or that a question be settled before it produces output. The system produces its three artifacts at every access level, including zero.

Conflicts are retained

When two sources give different figures for the same cell, both are kept with their provenance and no winner is picked. A pipeline that cleans conflicting values destroys the raw material the product is made of.

Three trust zones and a one-way door

A single confidence column is a label and can be ignored. This is a firewall.

Zone L · Leads

Everything from public or secondary sourcing lives here and only here, graded on two independent Admiralty axes. A lead's only legitimate output is a Collection Requirement. Zone L has no join path into the canonical tables. That is structural rather than procedural.

Zone R · Raw authoritative

Only primary material actually collected, with full chain of custody. Ingested, not yet trusted for arithmetic.

Zone V · Validated

A record enters only when a named person or a documented deterministic rule validates it against the source or ties it to a control total. Only this zone feeds anything presented as a figure.

Weakest-link aggregation. Any total inherits the weakest trust state among its inputs. If one period's contribution is lead-grade, the whole total displays as lead-grade. That prevents a good-looking aggregate from concealing a weak input, and it is what makes it safe to show partial work to an audience that will not read footnotes.

What the method got wrong, kept on purpose

All three share a shape: a structural pattern was real, the interpretation laid over it was invented, and the result was coherent. Coherence is not evidence.

PREMISE

Wrong platform

Cost category proportions were taken from exhibit columns assumed to be carrier columns. They were Columbia-class submarine columns. The row structure is identical on every line of that appropriation, which is why the extract passed inspection. The model had the two largest categories almost exactly inverted and would have been published with an ESTIMATE tag and a confidence score of 0.55.

INTERPRETATION

Inferring a footnote

A dagger on three cost rows was read as marking government furnished content, because those are the GFE-heavy categories and the pattern fit. The exhibit defines it in place: it indicates the presence of a further exhibit. Plausible, convenient, consistent with everything else, and wrong.

METHOD

Summing announcements

A contract was reconstructed by adding up announced modifications and carried at roughly a quarter of its real value. Announcements have a dollar threshold, omit incremental funding, and cannot net deobligations. The error was knowable without new data: the residual it created had been written down and given a narrative label instead of a test.

The rule taken from all three. A press release is an announcement, never a record. And the corollary that matters more: a large unexplained residual is a finding, not a remainder. When a reconciliation leaves a gap and the gap gets a narrative label, the label is a hypothesis that needs a test.

Where AI sits, and the one rule everything follows

No AI output writes directly to a trusted table. Every role writes to a candidate location with a confidence grade and full provenance: what it looked at, what rule or model produced the output, and when. A person, or a deterministic rule for mechanical checks, promotes a candidate. More AI roles means more candidates arriving at the gate, never a looser gate.

Investigator

Unknowns in, hypotheses and collection requirements out. Never asserts a value.

Reader

Documents in, typed knowledge out. Never extracts a cell without its coordinate chain and every legend on its page.

Tie-out

Deterministic. No model, ever. If a check needs judgment it emits a flag and stops.

Challenger

Tries to break a claim. Sets the risk flags. Never promotes anything.

Attributor

Identity across systems with no shared key. Scored candidates only, never a silent merge.

Briefer

Audience-specific explanation. Never introduces a figure that is not in the ledger.

The honest limit, stated as a design property. The gate catches the error classes already seen, and a novel one only where the negative vocabulary reaches. The miss log is the mechanism by which that limit moves: when something gets through and is caught later, it becomes a row recording what the gate saw, what it allowed, and which flag should have fired.
